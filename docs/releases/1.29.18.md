# HCOneButton v1.29.18

## Keep shooting while you are in range.

> **Hunters no longer get pushed into melee while the target is still within Auto Shot range.**

Version `1.29.18` fixes a regression introduced in `1.29.16`. During some fights, the Advisor could switch to melee suggestions even though the target was still in shooting range, and Auto Shot could not be resumed from the Advisor.

## Fixed — Auto Shot at range

- When Auto Shot, or a ranged shot used to check range, reports the target in range, the Advisor now treats it as a ranged target. A melee-range report from Raptor Strike or Wing Clip can no longer override it.
- **ENABLE MELEE** and **Raptor Strike** are no longer suggested while you can shoot, and **RESUME AUTO SHOT** appears again when ranged fire has stopped.
- Melee guidance still applies in the dead zone and in actual melee. Range reports from Raptor Strike or Wing Clip still take precedence over older combat-log hits.

**Example:** a mob hits you, you step back to shooting range and stop. The Advisor asks you to press BASE once to resume Auto Shot instead of asking you to enable melee.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, action slots, permanent bindings, combat history and learned adjustments are preserved.
- BASE remains a single start/recovery press, not a key to spam. Each action still requires your click/key press.
- Other class policies, including the Rogue opener fix in `1.29.17`, remain unchanged.

## Validation and limitations

- **57 Lua files**, **44 Lua regression suites**, **58 TOC references** and **19 offline release-tool tests** pass; the addon package contains **62 files**.
- **62 Hunter checks**, including **4 new checks**, cover the priority of ranged over melee range checks, Auto Shot recovery and the transition back to melee in the dead zone.
- The fix was confirmed in game: Auto Shot resumes at shooting range and the Advisor no longer requests melee there. The automated checks use simulated client range results, and range reporting can vary by target size.
