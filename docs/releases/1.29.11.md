# HCOneButton v1.29.11

## A more aggressive Priest, from the first talent point.

> **More room for Mind Blast, useful DoTs and learned fillers. The Priest now keeps casting when it makes sense, without switching to the wand halfway through an otherwise healthy fight.**

Version `1.29.11` improves Priest leveling priorities, from early Smite gameplay to learned Mind Flay and mixed talent builds. Damage has a stronger place in the rotation while recovery mana and emergency handling remain protected.

## Improved — Keep the damage flowing

- **Mind Blast and useful fillers take priority over ordinary wand fallback.** The Priest favors worthwhile Shadow Word: Pain setup, burst, then learned Mind Flay or Smite. Holy Fire remains available when learned, usable and supported by enough remaining target lifetime.
- **Your first Shadow talent point no longer removes Smite.** Early Shadow Priests can keep casting between Mind Blast cooldowns before learning Mind Flay. Learned actives remain eligible in mixed builds, subject to native usability.
- **Spirit Tap no longer forces an early switch to wanding.** Crossing 55% target health or 52% mana is no longer enough to make Shoot dominate the rotation.
- Wand conservation receives higher priority at **25% mana or below**, or against a target at **20% health or below** when its remaining lifetime is estimated at four seconds or less, or unavailable. Otherwise, wanding remains a fallback after useful, affordable spells.

**Example:** an early Shadow Priest opens with Mind Blast, applies a useful Shadow Word: Pain and casts Smite while Mind Blast is on cooldown. The first Shadow talent point does not leave a gap in the rotation. The wand becomes the preferred finish when the target is nearly defeated or mana needs conserving.

## Improved — Spend mana with a recovery reserve

- When available, **the learned spell's current mana cost** determines whether a damage cast fits the budget. The default post-cast reserve is 15% of maximum mana, rising to 25% under pressure; a readable usable Shield/heal cost can raise that reserve further.
- Missing cost data uses conservative percentage checks instead of assuming a spell is free. Native cooldowns and spell requirements still apply.
- **Shield allows closer-range offensive casting.** Unsafe unshielded casts/channels remain restricted, and spells are skipped when they cannot land in time.
- Active Pain and Holy Fire DoTs are not overwritten unnecessarily. Their setup requires enough remaining target lifetime to be worthwhile.
- Without an eligible spell or usable wand, the Advisor waits instead of re-offering a BASE spell that its mana or casting-safety checks rejected.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset or new option required.** Existing settings, HUD positions, combat history, learned adjustments and ON/OFF preferences are preserved.
- Necessary healing and emergency control retain their existing safety handling. Real casts, channels and bandages still receive **LET IT FINISH** protection.
- The existing **WAND ACTIVE — LET IT RUN** feedback remains intact. Start Shoot once, then let the active auto-repeat continue.
- Other class policies, action slots, bindings and secure macros are unchanged. All protected actions require the player's click/key press through WoW's secure system.
- More offensive casting consumes more mana and can mean more drinking between pulls. Damage and overall leveling speed still depend on gear, targets and recovery downtime.
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2` and telemetry contract `1` are unchanged.

Use `/hcob options` for existing preferences, `/hcob tuning status` to inspect local learning, and `/hcob errors` when reporting a problem.
