# HCOneButton v1.29.10

## Let your wand work. Keep the next spell in focus.

> **Priest, Mage and Warlock now recognize when Shoot is already running: one start, clear active-wand feedback, and no repeated requests competing with the next useful spell.**

Version `1.29.10` improves wand combat guidance during leveling. The Advisor distinguishes starting your wand from continuing an existing auto-repeat, while preserving healing, control and class-specific priorities.

## Fixed — Repeated Shoot requests

- **An active wand no longer produces repeated Shoot suggestions.** When wanding remains the preferred action, the Advisor displays **WAND ACTIVE — LET IT RUN** without highlighting Shoot again.
- The active state stays consistent through the wand's cooldown cycle, avoiding repeated **BASE READY / BASE NOT READY** changes while Shoot is already running.
- A stale Shoot suggestion disappears as soon as the addon observes auto-repeat starting. When it stops, an appropriate new start can be suggested again.
- The shared fallback also recognizes active wand use on **Mage and Destruction Warlock**, even when their BASE button contains a normal damage spell.

## Improved — Clearer wand-to-spell guidance

- **Start Shoot with one click or key press.** Advisor hints, BASE feedback, tooltip and glow now reflect that behavior instead of inviting repeated wand input.
- **Healing, control, procs and offensive spells remain eligible.** Wanding itself is not treated as a cast/channel that freezes the rotation. The existing mana-efficiency priorities remain intact.
- Actual casts, channels and bandages still receive **LET IT FINISH** protection until they complete or are interrupted.
- Out-of-range warnings remain visible. An unavailable start shows **WAND NOT READY — WAIT / RECOVER**; missing targets and disabled Smart HUD no longer produce wand-spam instructions.

**Example:** your Priest applies a useful DoT and starts Shoot once. The Advisor shows **WAND ACTIVE — LET IT RUN** while wanding is preferred. If Shield or a heal becomes the next priority, that spell replaces the wand message. Once a real healing cast begins, **LET IT FINISH** keeps the next suggestion from interrupting it.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Keep your settings, HUD position, combat history, learned adjustments and ON/OFF preferences.
- No new option, binding or window. Existing action slots, class priority thresholds, recovery buttons, Bag Quick Delete and Local Adaptive Tuning remain available.
- Native spell macros and existing rank-selection behavior are unchanged. The addon does not add unconditional cast cancellation or automatically start/stop spells.
- All protected actions still require the player's click/key press through WoW's secure system. Native cooldowns, range and spell requirements still apply.
- Hunter Auto Shot and the other classes retain their existing behavior. Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2` and telemetry contract `1` are unchanged.

Use `/hcob options` for your existing preferences and `/hcob errors` when reporting a problem.
