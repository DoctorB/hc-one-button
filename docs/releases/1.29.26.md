# HCOneButton v1.29.26

## One cast per DoT — now for Corruption and Immolate too.

> **Cast-time DoTs are no longer cast twice in a row.**

Version `1.29.26` completes the double-DoT fix of `1.29.25`. That release covered the moment after a cast succeeds, but low-rank Corruption and Immolate have a 2-second cast: the cast bar ends a moment *before* the success is confirmed. In that short gap the Advisor saw neither a cast in progress nor the debuff and showed the same DoT again, so a key press (or an external pixel reader that keeps pressing the shown slot) cast it a second time.

## Fixed — Cast-time DoTs cast twice

- **The window opens when the cast starts.** As soon as you start casting a DoT, it counts as applied on that target for the cast time plus 1.5 seconds. When the real debuff appears, its actual timer takes over.
- **Failed casts are offered again.** If the cast is interrupted or fails, the DoT is recommended again right away. An extra key press rejected during the cast does not count as a failure.
- **Resists and target switches behave as in 1.29.25.** A resisted DoT is offered again when the window ends; a new target still gets its own DoTs at once.
- **Covers every cast-time debuff**, including Corruption and Immolate at low rank and Polymorph. Instant debuffs keep the 1.29.25 behavior.

**Example:** a level 7 Warlock casts Corruption (2-second cast). Before, Corruption came back on screen right as the cast bar finished and a second Corruption went out on a target that already had it. Now the Advisor moves straight to the next spell.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, saved positions, slot order, permanent bindings, combat history and learned adjustments are preserved.
- The Diagnostic Pixel is unchanged, so external pixel readers keep working as before.
- Protected actions still require your click or key press.

## Validation and limitations

- **57 Lua files**, **48 Lua regression suites** (including 6 new cast-start window checks) and **58 TOC references** pass.
- The fix is based on Warlock combat logs from `1.29.25`, where Corruption was cast twice in 14 of 43 fights and Immolate in 6 of 11. It has not yet been checked in game.
