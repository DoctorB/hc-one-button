# HCOneButton v1.29.24

## A cleaner HUD, and the next spell you can't miss — checked in game.

> **New flat dark look, and the recommended Action Panel slot now really stands out.**

Version `1.29.24` brings the HUD restyle, now checked in game. Nothing moves: icons sit on flat, dark slots with a thin frame in the state color, and the spell the Advisor wants is unmistakable on the Action Panel: it glows, pulses and grows slightly while the other slots step back. The code is the same as `1.29.23`, published again with its in-game check.

## Improved — Action Panel highlight

- **Ring, glow and a bigger icon.** The recommended slot gets a gold ring and a smooth pulsing glow, with a brighter flash when the recommendation changes. Its icon grows slightly over its own frame, never into the next slot.
- **Focus.** While a slot is recommended, the other slots dim so your eye goes straight to the right one.
- **Keys on the icons.** Every learned slot prints its binding in short form, as on Blizzard action bars: `S4` for SHIFT-4, `CS1` for CTRL-SHIFT-1, `M4` for mouse button 4. The recommended key turns gold.

## Improved — Flat dark theme

- **Cropped icons on flat slots** for BASE, the Advisor, the Action Panel and the Survival Strip, with a thin frame that takes the state color (range, cooldown, usable, recommended).
- **Advisor accent.** A thin stripe on the left edge and the icon frame follow the badge color: red for danger, amber for caution, blue or green for normal states. The glow now surrounds the icon only.
- **Panels** keep a neutral 1px frame; caution and danger still color it.

**Example:** in a fight the Advisor switches to Shadow Bolt: slot `04` flashes, its gold ring pulses, `S4` turns gold on the icon and the other slots dim. Press SHIFT-4 or click it.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Layout, sizes, saved positions, slot order, permanent bindings, combat history and learned adjustments are preserved.
- The Diagnostic Pixel is unchanged, so external pixel readers keep working as before.
- Protected actions still require your click or key press.

## Validation and limitations

- **57 Lua files**, **48 Lua regression suites** (including 31 Action Panel highlight checks) and **58 TOC references** pass.
- The new look has been checked in game.
- The `1.29.21` and `1.29.22` Warlock changes (single-press BASE, leveling, pull started by BASE) have also been checked in game.
