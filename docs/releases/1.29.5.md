# HCOneButton v1.29.5

## Sharper Rogue leveling. Clearer next steps.

> **Make better use of energy and combo points while leveling, give Gouge a real recovery window, and get a clear visual and sound cue when your attacks need a manual restart.**

Version `1.29.5` revisits the Rogue baseline using issues found in real leveling logs. The focus is useful decisions with the abilities and talents you have now, not an assumed endgame build or waiting for Adaptive Tuning to make the rotation usable.

## Improved — A Rogue baseline built for leveling

- **Talents matter from the first point:** energy costs and relevant timing decisions account for invested talents, including Improved Sinister Strike, Improved Eviscerate, Improved Slice and Dice and Improved Gouge.
- **Use combo points before the fight is over:** Eviscerate can be suggested earlier in appropriate finishing windows. Required finishers remain protected from tuning; builders no longer disappear simply because the target is below 20% health.
- **Less preparation on short fights:** Slice and Dice needs evidence that the target will live long enough, useful buff duration and enough energy left for the next builder. An active buff is not refreshed early.
- **Useful with Combat logger OFF:** a small, temporary target-health trend supports lifetime decisions without requiring saved combat logs.
- **Learned abilities stay available:** Hemorrhage, Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush are recognized in mixed talent builds, not only when their tree has the most points. Localized name casting continues to use learned spell ranks.
- **Keep fighting when conditions allow:** moderate caution and healthy two-target pulls can retain offensive suggestions; low-health emergencies and dangerous multi-pulls still take priority.

## Fixed — Give Gouge room to work

- Gouge's energy-recovery opportunity now requires enough energy to actually use it. It is situational control, not a mandatory cooldown on every mob.
- Planned recovery checks solo pressure, target lifetime and known Rogue damage-over-time effects that would break the pause.
- Gouge's secure action stops auto-attacks. The Advisor respects its actual control window and clears the next action while waiting, with a short cast-to-aura grace rather than assuming every attempt landed.
- A missed Gouge, broken control or a target change cannot leave a full-duration phantom pause. The hold yields at 80 energy, expiry or the existing emergency gates.
- An affordable, in-range Gouge can provide an interrupt fallback when Kick is unavailable and the target-facing condition permits it.

## New — A restart request you can see and hear

If no ability is available and the client confirms that melee auto-attack is stopped, the Advisor displays a prominent amber notice:

**ATTACK STOPPED — PRESS BUTTON4 — ONCE TO RESUME ATTACK**

- The notice shows **your actual BASE binding**. With no binding, it asks you to click BASE once.
- Large outlined text, a high-contrast background and a slowly pulsing border occupy the existing Advisor area, following HUD position and scale without covering health, energy or DPS.
- A distinct sound accompanies a new notice when **Alert sounds** is enabled. It plays once, with at least five seconds between restart cues; it does not loop while the notice remains visible.
- The notice clears when attacks resume or another recommendation takes over. Stealth, control and healing waits are not requests to press again; uncertain range or attack-state data is not treated as a confirmed stop.
- Energy waits say **WAIT FOR ENERGY**, instead of the old generic BASE-spam instruction. You do not need to watch the diagnostic pixel to understand the restart request.

**Example:** start outside Stealth, select a normal mob, move into melee range and press BASE once to begin auto-attacks. Follow the supported builder and finisher suggestions. If Gouge creates a recovery window, let it last. When combat should resume, use the next offensive action; if attacks remain stopped and no ability is available, the large notice and sound ask for one BASE press. If Stealth has already been applied, use an appropriate opener: BASE does not deliberately break it.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Keep SavedVariables to preserve settings, HUD positions, combat history, tuning data and ON/OFF preferences.
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2` and telemetry contract `1` are unchanged. This is a Rogue baseline and feedback update, not a new learner or a complete mathematical simulation of every talent.
- Existing fixed action slots, bindings, DPS/aggro display and Diagnostic Pixel Protocol V3 encoding remain unchanged. Sinister Strike and Hemorrhage already occupy slots 1 and 2; the manual restart cue itself has no executable slot.
- If you use an external reader, it must respect black/no-action pauses and not send independent BASE presses alongside another suggestion. Such presses can break Gouge or consume energy reserved for a finisher; this addon does not coordinate or change external software.
- Protected actions still require player input. Movement, facing, targets and unsupported abilities remain manual. No new Ambush/Backstab automation, external executable or data upload is introduced.

Use **Options → Alert sounds** to enable or mute the restart cue together with existing alerts. Check `/hcob errors` after testing. Existing `/hcob tuning status` and `/hcob tuning on|off` commands remain available; their saved preferences are preserved.

## Validation and limitations

- 47/47 addon Lua files parse; 30/30 Lua regression harnesses pass.
- The Rogue harness covers 110 checks; the restart HUD/audio harness covers 78 checks, including timing, bindings, visibility, layout, mute/unmute and missing API handling.
- 48/48 TOC references resolve; runtime versions and packaged documentation match.
- 19/19 offline release-tool tests pass, with shared regression coverage across all nine classes.
- **In-game smoke test passed:** the user tested `1.29.5` and reported that everything looks correct. This is not a claim that every class, HUD/audio setting or regression scenario was exercised.

Finishing windows are conservative leveling heuristics, not exact lethal-damage predictions. Automated tests cannot reproduce every WoW event ordering, secure-input interaction or audio/visual setting, and do not establish a measured DPS or time-to-kill improvement. Local packaging does not publish to CurseForge.
