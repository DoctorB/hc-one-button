# HCOneButton v1.29.14

## Keep your Rage for the attack you chose.

> **Cleaner Warrior inputs and smarter Rage protection: BASE stays consistent, and maintenance spells stop competing for Rage already committed to your next strike.**

Version `1.29.14` builds on offensive Warrior leveling with two focused fixes. Holding a modifier no longer changes what BASE does, and the Advisor accounts for the cost of an already queued Heroic Strike or Cleave before suggesting a maintenance buff or debuff.

## Fixed — One BASE action, with or without modifiers

- **SHIFT, CTRL and ALT no longer redirect Warrior BASE** to Battle Shout, Thunder Clap or another spell. Every modifier combination keeps the same BASE action.
- **Dedicated spell actions are unchanged.** Use the Action Panel icons or their existing slot bindings for Battle Shout, Heroic Strike and the other abilities.
- Removed obsolete Warrior modifier shortcuts from recommendation hints. The BASE tooltip and `/hcob plan` explain the new behavior.
- Other classes keep their existing BASE modifier actions.

## Improved — Protect the Rage committed to your queued strike

- Once **Heroic Strike or Cleave is queued**, Battle Shout, Rend, Sunder Armor, Thunder Clap and Demoralizing Shout are suggested only when there is enough Rage for both the queued strike and the maintenance spell.
- Costs use the learned, localized spell and the client's current talent discounts when available, with conservative undiscounted costs as a fallback.
- If queuing a strike makes an already displayed maintenance suggestion unaffordable, that hint clears immediately.
- This is **not a blanket pause**: funded maintenance remains available, while core attacks, procs, interrupts and critical emergencies retain their priorities.
- The saved Heroic Strike base, learned-kit thresholds, swing window and local tuning coefficients are unchanged.

**Example:** your Warrior has 23 Rage and a Heroic Strike costing 15 is already queued. An otherwise eligible Battle Shout costing 10 is deferred because the two actions need 25 Rage together. With 25 Rage, both can be funded. Holding SHIFT while pressing BASE no longer casts Battle Shout through the old shortcut.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, positions, combat history, learned adjustments, fixed action slots and permanent bindings are preserved.
- **Warrior shortcut change:** modifier+BASE now performs BASE. Use the dedicated spell bindings or icons instead; `/hcob plan` shows the updated contract.
- With default bindings, Heroic Strike remains on **SHIFT+4** and Battle Shout on **SHIFT+8**. Customized slot bindings are preserved.
- Critical-health safeguards and the offensive warning policy remain in place. Priest, the other class rotations and the independent party-healing panel retain their existing behavior.
- Protected actions still require your click/key press. The queue budget governs Advisor maintenance suggestions; manual casts, the optional BASE Rend opener and higher-priority attacks can still spend Rage.

## Validation and limitations

- **56/56** addon Lua files parse; **40/40** Lua regression harnesses pass.
- **134 focused input/budget checks** cover every BASE modifier combination, learned costs, talent discounts, malformed API fallbacks, both queued strikes, stale hints and combat-lockdown boundaries.
- The existing **491 Warrior leveling checks** and all-class, healing, aura, settings and binding regressions pass.
- **19/19** offline release-tool tests pass; **57/57** TOC references resolve. The package contains **61 files** with matching metadata and documentation.
- Automated checks use simulated client APIs. These fixes remove avoidable input and budget conflicts; they do not guarantee that every queued strike lands or establish a measured DPS increase.
