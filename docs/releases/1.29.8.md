# HCOneButton v1.29.8

## Free a bag slot without dragging items out of your bags.

> **New optional Bag Quick Delete: Ctrl + Alt + right-click a bag item, with immediate gray-stack deletion and confirmation for everything above gray. Works with standard bags and ElvUI bags—ElvUI is not required.**

Version `1.29.8` adds a small, practical convenience for leveling: remove an unwanted stack directly where it sits in your bags. Your combat recommendations, tuning and existing settings stay intact.

## New — Bag Quick Delete for every class

- Enable **Quick delete bag items** under **Appearance and behavior** in `/hcob options`. It is **OFF by default**, available to every class and saved across reload/logout.
- Outside combat, hold **Ctrl + Alt**, without Shift, and hover a supported bag item. A red **DEL** overlay shows the shortcut is armed. **Right-click that item** to request deletion of its entire stack.
- **Gray / poor:** the selected stack is deleted immediately, without an additional addon confirmation.
- **White / common and higher:** a confirmation names the item and quantity before the addon picks it up. Cancel or Escape leaves it untouched. Mandatory client restrictions and native confirmations are not bypassed.
- **Standard WoW bags and ElvUI bags** are supported integration targets. No ElvUI dependency, separate bag window, external executable or drag-out gesture is needed.
- A normal right-click retains its existing use/equip behavior. Left/middle and extra mouse buttons pass through to the original bag widget.

**Example:** your backpack is full and contains a stack of eight gray Broken Fangs. Enable the option, hold Ctrl + Alt until **DEL** appears over that stack, and right-click once. The whole stack is removed. Doing the same with a white material first opens a confirmation showing its quantity.

## Safety — One deliberate action, one stack

- Only the backpack and four equipped bags are eligible. Bank slots, equipped items and unrecognized bag-addon widgets are not intercepted.
- Items identified as quest items, the Hearthstone, locked items, containers still holding loot and items with incomplete metadata are protected. An occupied cursor or active spell targeting also blocks the operation.
- Item identity, bag, slot and quantity are checked again before deletion. Inventory changes invalidate an open confirmation; an old confirmation cannot silently follow an item to another slot or delete a replacement.
- Combat, death, logout, Cancel, Escape and disabling the option cancel pending addon confirmations. No timer, inventory scan or event performs deletion, and there is no bulk cleanup.
- If mouse integration is unavailable, this convenience disables itself for the session. Original bag click handlers are not replaced.

**Destruction is not selling. The entire selected stack is destroyed, and HCOneButton cannot undo it.** Confirm only items you intend to lose; a useful crafting material is not automatically protected merely because you may need it later. If the client blocks deletion after pickup, return the item from the cursor to your bag; the addon does not retry or clear it automatically.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Existing settings, HUD positions, combat history, learned adjustments and ON/OFF choices are preserved.
- The new preference is account-wide (`HCOB_DB.bagQuickDelete`) and starts OFF. **Reset defaults** switches it OFF again and closes any pending addon confirmation.
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2`, telemetry contract `1`, combat rotations, deterministic action slots and Diagnostic Pixel V3 encoding are unchanged.
- Diagnostic pixels and external readers remain documented exclusively for **passive diagnostics**. Combat actions require the player's click/key press through WoW's secure system.

Use `/hcob options` to enable the feature and `/hcob errors` when reporting a problem. Other bag replacements are not guaranteed compatible; unsupported widgets retain their normal behavior.
