# HCOneButton v1.29.21

## Press once, let the pet and the Advisor work.

> **Warlock BASE is now a single press, and Warlock leveling gets smarter pet, Soul Shard and casting decisions.**

Version `1.29.21` stops asking Warlocks to spam BASE. BASE starts your wand and sends your pet; every spell, Shadow Bolt included, comes from the Advisor on its own slot. The Advisor also keeps you casting while your pet holds the mob, farms Soul Shards with Drain Soul and keeps your Voidwalker alive with Health Funnel.

## Improved — Warlock single-press BASE

- **No more Shadow Bolt spam.** With a wand, BASE sends the pet (in combat) and starts the wand in every talent tree, Destruction included. Without a wand, BASE only sends the pet. Shadow Bolt and every other spell are suggested by the Advisor on their fixed slots, so extra BASE presses can no longer cast at the wrong moment.
- **Pet idle? Press once.** When your pet is not attacking your target for more than a second in a single-mob fight, the amber `PET NOT ATTACKING / PRESS <your BASE key> ONCE` notice appears. It stays quiet in multi-mob fights, where the pet may be tanking another mob on purpose.
- **Clear waits without a wand.** In combat the Advisor shows `LET DOTS AND PET WORK` when nothing is worth casting; before a pull without mana for an opener it shows `NO OPENER READY`. Neither asks for BASE.

## Improved — Warlock leveling

- **Keep casting next to your pet.** Within about 10 yards of a mob your pet is holding, Shadow Bolt, Immolate, wand and Drain Soul stay available. They are skipped only when the mob is on you or not held.
- **Soul Shards.** Drain Soul finishes low-health targets until you hold five shards. Shadowburn keeps two shards in reserve unless the fight is dangerous.
- **Health Funnel.** A tanking pet at 40% health or less gets Health Funnel while your own health can pay for it. Health Funnel is the new Warlock slot `16`.
- **Openers and fillers.** Before a pull the Advisor suggests a Shadow Bolt opener (Corruption on low mana, Life Tap first when mana is low and health high). Without a wand Shadow Bolt remains available on lower mana and on low-health targets.
- **Spell Lock** is no longer suggested while it is on cooldown.
- **Before the pull,** a missing pet points at Summon Voidwalker (Summon Imp without Soul Shards) and a hurt pet at Health Funnel.

**Example:** select a mob and cast the suggested Shadow Bolt. As it lands, the amber notice asks you to press BASE once: the pet charges in and the wand starts. Follow the highlighted DoTs; near the end the Advisor switches to Drain Soul for a shard.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, existing action slots, permanent bindings, combat history and learned adjustments are preserved. Warlocks gain slot `16` at the end of their layout.
- Without a wand, cast Shadow Bolt from its slot (`04`) when suggested: BASE no longer casts it.
- Protected actions still require your click or key press.
