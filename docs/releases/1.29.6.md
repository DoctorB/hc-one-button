# HCOneButton v1.29.6

## Better openings. Better preparation. Clearer interrupts.

> **Put your main-hand dagger to work from Stealth, catch neglected weapon preparation before a pull, and get interrupt advice that follows the target's cast more closely.**

Version `1.29.6` builds on the Rogue leveling improvements from the previous release. It adds practical guidance for the weapons, learned abilities and talents your character has now, while keeping positioning and every combat action in your hands.

## New — Dagger-aware Stealth openings

- **Ambush joins the Advisor:** when learned, affordable and ready, it becomes a burst-opening option with a confirmed **main-hand dagger**. A dagger in the off hand alone does not qualify.
- **Know where to stand:** `AMBUSH - BEHIND` and `GARROTE - BEHIND` make the positional requirement explicit. You move behind the target and execute the opener yourself.
- **Keep useful alternatives:** Garrote remains a bleed option, with a reminder that its damage takes time and prevents a planned Gouge recovery pause. Cheap Shot keeps opening-control priority against difficult targets when available.
- **Relevant talents count:** Improved Ambush and Opportunity influence Ambush preference; Dirty Deeds is reflected in fallback costs for Garrote and Cheap Shot. Actual client-reported costs take precedence.
- **Preserve your existing keys:** Ambush is appended at Rogue slot **18**, default **CTRL+SHIFT+8**. Slots 1–17 keep their assignments. Stealth-only opener buttons do not issue an auto-attack before the cast.

If no opener is usable, the Advisor says **CHECK OPENER** instead of asking for BASE: check your learned abilities, energy, weapon and position.

## New — Quiet preparation advice

A small amber **CHECK GEAR** badge appears out of combat when there is actionable equipment advice. Hover **BASE** to read the details.

- Warn when an equipped weapon skill is at least **10 points** behind its readable maximum.
- Once poisons are learned at level **20+**, identify equipped hands missing a temporary weapon coating.
- Accept a valid sharpening stone or other temporary coating instead of incorrectly claiming that the weapon is uncoated.
- Use the existing persisted **Options → Pre-pull safety gate** toggle: no new setting to configure.
- Never block **PULL READY**, replace the suggested action, play a new sound or repeat warnings in chat.

This is a preparation reminder, not a prediction of your miss rate or a claim that every miss has the same cause. Unknown equipment or skill data does not produce invented advice.

## Improved — More reliable interrupt guidance

- Use live target cast/channel timing and interruptibility when the client supplies them, including cast delays and updates.
- Clear obsolete cast information after confirmed stops, failures and target changes; avoid confusing a late stop with a newer cast of the same spell.
- Suppress interrupt suggestions in the final **0.15 seconds**. Rogue Kick also respects known out-of-range results and explicit direct-interrupt immunity.
- Retain a bounded combat-event fallback for Classic Era targets without readable cast APIs.
- Keep suitable class-owned control alternatives: immunity to a direct interrupt does not automatically mean immunity to Gouge or a stun. The addon does not guarantee that control will land.

The shared cast-tracking improvements apply to all nine supported classes; Rogue-specific action selection stays in the Rogue module.

**Example:** your Rogue has learned Ambush and has a main-hand dagger equipped. Before a safe pull, `CHECK GEAR` may point out a weapon skill still at 65/75. After preparing, enter Stealth, move behind the target and use the Ambush suggestion when available. Continue following builders and finishers; if the enemy begins a readable, interruptible cast in range, Kick can take priority. You perform each action yourself.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, HUD positions, combat history, tuning data and ON/OFF choices are preserved.
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2` and telemetry contract `1` remain unchanged. This is not a new learning model or a complete talent/damage simulator.
- Existing slot assignments, the DPS/aggro meter and Diagnostic Pixel V3 encoding are preserved; Rogue gains slot 18.
- The pixel and any external reader are referenced exclusively for **passive diagnostics**. HCOneButton recommends actions; protected actions require a player click or key press through WoW's secure system.
- Movement, facing, equipment changes and casts remain manual. No external executable, automated input or gameplay-data upload is introduced.

Use **Options → Pre-pull safety gate** to control the preparation notice, and check `/hcob errors` after testing. Existing `/hcob tuning status` and `/hcob tuning on|off` commands are unchanged.

## Validation and limitations

- Two review and refinement cycles completed.
- **48/48** addon Lua files parse; **31/31** Lua regression harnesses pass.
- **151 Rogue checks**, **85 Advisor UI/hint/audio checks** and **81 target-cast checks**, plus shared regressions across all nine classes.
- **49/49** TOC references resolve; **19/19** offline release-tool tests pass. Runtime versions and packaged documentation match.
- **In-game validation of 1.29.6 is pending.** The maintainer explicitly chose publication as an ordinary Release with this limitation disclosed. The previous version's smoke test is not validation of these additions.

Automated tests cannot reproduce every Classic Era event ordering, secure-input interaction or UI setting. Rear positioning is not inferred reliably, unknown NPC immunity is not invented, and some target channels may remain unobservable. No measured DPS or time-to-kill improvement is claimed.
