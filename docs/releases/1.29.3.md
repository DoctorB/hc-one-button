# HCOneButton v1.29.3

## Watch your damage. Keep an eye on aggro.

> **Your combat HUD now shows live threat right beside DPS: see when you are approaching the aggro threshold, when the target is on you and when your pet is holding it.**

Version `1.29.3` adds a compact aggro meter for all nine supported classes, without another window to manage. Your existing DPS display gains a second, color-coded row for the current hostile NPC target.

## New — DPS / aggro meter

- **Live player threat:** a percentage and compact bar show your progress toward the client's aggro threshold. `100%` is that threshold, not your share of group damage.
- **Readable states:** teal `LOW`, amber `HIGH` at 85% or elevated client threat status, and red `AGGRO` when the client reports that you hold aggro. Having aggro is normal when intentionally tanking or fighting solo.
- **Pet-aware:** `PET` or `HIGH / PET` indicates that your pet holds the target. This includes pulls started by the pet before you enter combat. The percentage always refers to **you**, not your pet.
- **Honest when data is missing:** `THREAT --` means the client has not supplied a usable percentage. A known aggro/pet status can still appear; no percentage is invented.
- **Fresh target data:** changing or clearing targets, leaving combat and selecting friendly, player or dead targets clears the old percentage.
- **No logger required for threat:** the aggro row reads the client's threat APIs directly. DPS statistics retain their existing Combat logger dependency.

## Improved — One compact view of damage and threat

- The original DPS, recent average, damage and fight-duration row remains available.
- Enable or hide both rows through **Options → Combat data and learning → DPS / aggro meter**, or `/hcob dps on|off`.
- Your previous meter preference is preserved across upgrades, `/reload` and logout.
- The new row follows the shared HUD position and scale, with reserved space above the Action Panel and Survival strip.
- The meter does not intercept clicks and remains usable with the Advisor hidden.

**Example:** your pet is tanking and the HUD shows `THREAT 90% — HIGH / PET`. You are approaching the threshold for taking aggro. If the client then reports that you hold the target, the display changes to `AGGRO`.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Keep SavedVariables to preserve settings, HUD position, combat history and existing tuning data.
- Local Adaptive Tuning remains at learner revision `3`, adaptive schema `2`; no new coefficient migration is introduced.
- Class rotations, emergency protection, cast/range/aura rules, secure actions, bindings and Diagnostic Pixel Protocol V3 are unchanged.
- The meter is read-only: it never executes an action or changes learned priorities. Your existing Local Adaptive Tuning ON/OFF preference remains intact.

Use `/hcob dps on|off` to show or hide the combined meter, or open **Options → Combat data and learning → DPS / aggro meter**. Only the DPS statistics require Combat logger; live threat remains available with logging disabled.

## Validation and limitations

- 47/47 addon Lua files parse; all 26 Lua regression harnesses pass.
- 48/48 TOC references resolve; runtime versions and packaged documentation match.
- 19/19 offline release-tool tests pass.
- Threat tests cover 48 API states plus DPS history, target reset, pet pulls, unavailable data, saved visibility and HUD-scale/layout integration.
- The new aggro meter was tested in game and reported working. This smoke test does not represent exhaustive live coverage of every class and threat state.

This is a read-only **current-target** display, not a full raid threat ranking, an all-enemy monitor or a PvP predictor. It uses the client's available threat data rather than estimating threat from combat logs. Missing data legitimately leaves the percentage at `--`; `LOW` is not a guarantee of safety. Offline release tests do not establish successful CurseForge upload or moderation.
