# HCOneButton v1.29.15

## Spend Rage where it can do more.

> **Warrior leveling gains a live damage-per-Rage comparison: better-informed choices between Heroic Strike, Rend and Sunder Armor, fewer unnecessary defensive shouts, and a DPS clock that stops after the fight is won.**

Version `1.29.15` focuses on making Warrior recommendations respond to your current character and the enemy's remaining life. The new comparison follows selected Fight Club DPR principles while preserving practical priorities such as reactive attacks, finishing opportunities and avoiding wasted Rage at the cap.

## New — Character-aware Warrior efficiency

- Compare **Heroic Strike, early Rend and the first Sunder Armor application** using current weapon damage/speed, level, crit, actual learned ranks and native spell costs.
- Include **the white-swing Rage lost when queuing Heroic Strike**. Its full weapon hit is not incorrectly counted as entirely additional damage.
- Account for **invested Improved Rend and Impale**, and the talent discounts returned by the client. Leveling or changing talents refreshes the relevant inputs; an untrained rank is never assumed learned.
- Evaluate **Rend's remaining ticks** and **Sunder's benefit against remaining enemy health**, rather than relying exclusively on fixed priority scores.
- Show `est. DPR` in modeled recommendation reasons, with an explicit label when enemy armor is estimated.

**Example:** early in a durable solo fight, Sunder may be the better investment because several physical attacks remain. As that same enemy loses health, the benefit shrinks and Heroic Strike can become the better choice. Near the Rage cap or a likely finishing swing, immediate damage takes precedence over optional setup.

## Improved — Keep offense moving

- A healthy, equal-level normal enemy no longer triggers **Demoralizing Shout or Thunder Clap** merely because of its level. Normal mitigation requires confirmed melee reach and a multi-pull, elite opponent, or recent incoming melee at reduced health.
- Near-cap Heroic Strike remains available, while a likely finishing opportunity may use its actual affordable cost instead of keeping an unnecessary reserve.
- Core attacks, Overpower, Execute, critical-health safeguards and interrupts keep their priorities. Rend and Sunder do not consume the Rage reserved during Execute pooling.
- Existing swing timing and protection of Rage already committed to a queued Heroic Strike/Cleave remain intact.

## Fixed — Repeated debuffs and misleading post-kill timing

- Brief cast/aura update delays no longer invite repeated mitigation or Sunder requests. Existing same-name mitigation/Sunder debuffs from another caster are recognized; Expose Armor also prevents redundant Sunder suggestions.
- The **HUD DPS clock stops after confirmed deaths of all observed engaged enemies**, instead of continuing to dilute the result while WoW delays leaving combat.
- Real waits during a living fight still count, including swings, casts, resource waits and gaps before a new enemy in the same combat interval. Pet damage uses the same clock.
- Full combat duration and its original DPS remain available for learning and diagnostics; the new display metric is stored separately.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, positions, combat history, learned adjustments, deterministic action slots and permanent bindings are preserved.
- Your saved Heroic Strike base and Warrior options remain in place. `/hcob options` opens them; `/hcob log last` reports the last fight's combat and damage-window timing.
- The new DPR comparison covers **solo, single-target PvE without dual wield**. Group combat, PvP, multi-pulls, dual wield and incomplete inputs retain the existing scoring policy.
- Sunder remains a first-application policy, not an instruction to build five stacks. The optional BASE Rend opener is unchanged.
- Other class rotations and the independent party-healing panel retain their behavior. Protected actions still require your click/key press.

## Validation and limitations

- **57/57 addon Lua files**, **43/43 Lua regression suites**, **58/58 TOC references**, and **19/19 offline release-tool tests** pass. The verified package contains **62 files**.
- **173 focused DPR checks** cover levels, trained ranks, talent changes, weapon/cost inputs, changing candidate choices, fallback contexts and preserved priorities.
- Existing **491 Warrior leveling** and **134 input/budget checks** pass, alongside **66 mitigation** and **118 damage-timing checks**, all-class and party-healing regressions.
- This is a focused efficiency model, not a full Fight Club spreadsheet simulation. Unknown NPC armor uses a labeled estimate; enemy avoidance and future proc damage are not fabricated. No measured DPS increase is claimed.
- Damage totals still include overkill. A higher displayed DPS caused by excluding a confirmed post-kill tail is a timing correction, not additional damage dealt. Legacy fight records keep their original timing.
