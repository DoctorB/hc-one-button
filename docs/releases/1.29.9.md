# HCOneButton v1.29.9

## Recover between pulls without digging through your bags.

> **New FOOD and DRINK buttons bring between-pull recovery into your Survival strip: suitable supplies selected for you, clear recovery feedback, and every use kept under your control.**

Version `1.29.9` makes the pause between fights more convenient for leveling. Spend less time searching your bags, keep your emergency supplies for when they matter, and see when your character is eating or drinking directly in the Advisor.

## New — Food and water at your fingertips

- **FOOD and DRINK join the existing Survival strip.** Six buttons fit into the same panel, following your saved HUD position, scale and visibility choice. No separate window or bag addon is required.
- The addon selects the **strongest usable supported recovery tier** in your bags. Conjured food or water takes preference over ordinary supplies at the same tier; stronger ordinary supplies still take priority over weaker conjured ones.
- Common plain cooked foods are included, with the actual item level requirement checked so lower-level characters can use appropriate cooked alternatives.
- **Left-click once outside combat** to use the selected item. Quantities, availability and recovery labels remain visible. If both health and mana are low, food and water can be started with two separate manual clicks.
- DRINK is not applicable to Warriors and Rogues. Druids must leave their form manually before using recovery items.

**Example:** your Mage finishes a fight with missing health and mana. Click FOOD, then DRINK. The Advisor displays **EAT + DRINK — LET IT FINISH**, while the existing resource bars show your recovery. When your resources are full, or you move to interrupt recovery, normal guidance resumes.

## Improved — Recovery-aware guidance

- At **85% health or below**, usable food is highlighted before out-of-combat bandages or healing potions. Water can be highlighted below **60% mana**.
- Real eating/drinking auras produce **EATING**, **DRINKING** or **EAT + DRINK** guidance. The Advisor pauses its spell suggestion while the corresponding recovery is useful, including when no target is selected.
- Guidance resumes when recovery ends or the relevant resources are full; it does not wait for an already-unneeded food buff to expire. **Well Fed is not mistaken for active eating.**
- Combat emergencies retain their priority. Food and water never count as emergency healing stock and are never selected for use in combat.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Existing settings, HUD positions, combat history, learned adjustments and ON/OFF preferences are preserved. The existing **Survival consumables strip** option controls visibility for all six buttons.
- Item use always requires your **manual click** through WoW's secure system. There is no automatic purchase, conjuring, dismount, form change or item consumption.
- Active same-role recovery, a full corresponding resource and rapid repeated clicks prevent redundant use. Movement, mounting, death, casting/channeling and combat also guard recovery clicks.
- Selection is limited to supported plain Classic food and water. Buff food, raw cooking materials, alcohol and unsupported special refreshments are not selected; you can still use those items manually from your bags.
- Secure assignments stay frozen during combat and refresh afterward. Missing items clear their old assignments; incomplete item metadata waits for the game cache.
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2`, telemetry contract `1`, combat rotations, action slots and Diagnostic Pixel V3 encoding are unchanged. Diagnostic pixels and external readers remain documented exclusively for **passive diagnostics**; gameplay actions remain player-executed.

Use `/hcob options` to control strip visibility, `/hcob consumables` to inspect selected supplies, and `/hcob errors` when reporting a problem.
