# HCOneButton v1.29.12

## Help your party without losing your target.

> **A dedicated healing panel, your preferred mouse clicks and the highest learned spell ranks: group leveling gets a simpler way to keep teammates alive.**

Version `1.29.12` adds party click-healing and optional group-healing advice for **Priest, Paladin, Shaman and Druid**. Keep the enemy selected, see who needs help and choose the heal yourself.

## New — Your party, one click away

- **An independent party health panel** shows you and up to four teammates. Drag its title bar outside combat to place it wherever it fits your interface, independently of the main HUD.
- **Configure left, right, middle, Mouse 4 and Mouse 5 clicks**, with optional Shift, Ctrl or Alt. Assign a supported quick heal, direct heal, HoT or Shield; choose **None** to leave a combination inactive.
- **Your own bar remains visible while solo.** Check your assignments or heal yourself without joining a group. Teammate bars appear when you party up.
- Save panel visibility, position, position lock and click assignments **per character**. No ElvUI or additional healing addon is required.

## New — Group advice that leaves you in control

- The Advisor can show an injured teammate's **name, health and suggested spell**, together with the available click or mouseover key. The corresponding party bar is highlighted.
- Advice considers mana, spell range, movement, incoming heals and active HoTs/shields. Personal emergencies, interrupts and ongoing casts/channels keep their priority.
- **Group healing advice** is ON by default for supported healers and persists across reload/logout. Turn it OFF to keep manual healing without group suggestions; panel visibility has its own switch.
- Prefer the keyboard? Four dedicated **mouseover healing roles** support party and raid frames, or a friendly character under the cursor, without changing your hostile target. Keys are assigned by you, never automatically overwritten.

## Improved — Learn a rank, keep your clicks

- **The highest learned rank is used automatically.** You do not need to reassign a mouse button after visiting your trainer.
- Healing roles follow learned spell-family upgrades, such as Lesser Heal → Heal → Greater Heal. Only supported learned spells are offered for assignment.
- Middle and side mouse bindings apply only while hovering a party bar. Leaving or hiding it restores the underlying game bindings; permanent bindings and normal mouse-wheel scrolling are preserved.

**Example:** on a Priest, assign left-click to Renew and middle-click to Power Word: Shield. While keeping a mob selected, left-click Marco's party bar to apply Renew, or middle-click for Shield. A newly learned rank is used on the next click once the spell configuration updates outside combat. You always choose the teammate and perform the click.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Keep existing settings, HUD positions, combat history, learned adjustments and ON/OFF preferences. Existing Action Panel slots and permanent bindings remain intact.
- Open `/hcob options` → **Group healing - Party support** → **Party panel and click bindings...** to configure the panel. Use **Preview / move**, **Center panel** or **Reset clicks** as needed.
- Configure and move outside combat; the fixed-unit secure healing buttons remain available during combat. Closing the configuration window returns to Options, except when combat closes it.
- One modifier at a time: combined modifiers are inactive. The party panel covers **you + four party members**, not a raid grid; dedicated mouseover keys remain available in raids.
- All protected actions require a deliberate player click/key press. Hovering alone does not cast, and the addon never selects a healing target automatically. Native cooldowns, range, mana and spell restrictions still apply.
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2` and telemetry contract `1` are unchanged. Group-heal inputs remain separate from self-survival learning.
