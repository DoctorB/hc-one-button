# HCOneButton v1.29.4

## Your actions count. Your tuning is easier to understand.

> **Local Adaptive Tuning now keeps track of the spell you actually started—even when the Advisor has already moved on. A cleaner, expandable spell list shows what is observed, what is being compared and what has really changed.**

Version `1.29.4` focuses on trustworthy learning evidence and a clearer tuning inspector, with fixes prompted by real Warrior combat logs and regression checks across all nine supported classes.

## Fixed — Give executed spells their proper credit

- **Queued strikes stay connected:** Heroic Strike and Cleave retain their original suggestion until the swing executes. They are no longer mistaken for ignoring the Advisor's next recommendation.
- **Learned ranks are recognized:** queued-strike hits and misses use the correct spell identity instead of checking only the rank-1 ID.
- **Stable spell roles:** short aura/resource updates no longer split a single Battle Shout or Thunder Clap suggestion into separate classified and generic entries. The same attribution fix covers other classes.
- **Cast-aware tracking:** bounded pending snapshots survive valid cast holds and HUD transitions. Failed or interrupted attempts and target changes cannot become fresh comparison evidence; repeated failed inputs do not cancel a different in-progress cast or an armed swing.
- **No double counting:** interleaved cast confirmations are deduplicated, and repeated inputs are not additional learning samples.

## Improved — One spell, with the details when you want them

- The main inspector list shows **one entry per spell**, using its localized name.
- Click **`[+]`** to inspect separate roles and situations, including their chosen/alternative counts and priority corrections.
- **`VARIES`** means different recorded situations have different corrections. Expand the spell to see them; opposite corrections are not averaged into a misleading zero.
- Historical unclassified entries remain available as history, not repurposed as new comparative evidence.
- The existing PvE/PvP views, active-only filter, automatic refresh, baseline legend and two-step reset remain available.

## Improved — Know whether learning has enough evidence

- **OBSERVING:** collecting observations; no usable comparisons yet.
- **COMPARING:** comparisons exist, but no situation meets both sample requirements yet.
- **BASELINE:** at least one situation has enough evidence, with no current learned correction.
- **ADAPTED:** learned corrections are present. They apply only when tuning is ON and gameplay conditions match.

Eight eligible fights establish the minimum fight baseline; they do **not** guarantee a ready learner. Each situation still needs at least **four chosen and four alternative-choice fights**. With only one available action there is no comparison, and unchanged priorities can be the correct result.

**Example:** the Advisor suggests Heroic Strike, you queue it and Sunder Armor becomes the next suggestion. When Heroic Strike lands, its execution is credited to the original Heroic Strike decision—not recorded as a deviation from Sunder. The tuning list keeps Heroic Strike under one expandable entry.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Keep SavedVariables to preserve settings, positions, combat history, tuning ON/OFF and existing comparative data.
- Learner revision `4` retains adaptive schema `2`. Historical observations are not converted into comparisons, and evidence missed by old versions cannot be recovered retroactively.
- Emergency priorities, spell eligibility, cooldown/aura/range checks, cast holds and swing windows are unchanged. This update does not teach new rotation thresholds or promise a DPS increase.
- DPS/aggro display, secure actions, bindings and Diagnostic Pixel Protocol V3 remain unchanged.
- Learning stays local to the character. No upload, executable or account is required; protected gameplay actions still require player input.

Open **Options → Combat data and learning → View learned adjustments...** or use `/hcob tuning status`. `/hcob tuning on|off` preserves your preference across reload/logout; `/hcob tuning reset` clears only this character's learning.
