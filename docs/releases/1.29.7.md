# HCOneButton v1.29.7

## Optional extra loot. Clearer Rogue cues. More reliable learning samples.

> **Try Pick Pocket with your Stealth opener, leave confusing BASE-spam prompts behind, and let Local Adaptive Tuning learn from better-qualified combat samples across all nine classes.**

Version `1.29.7` follows up on real Rogue session data with practical quality-of-life improvements and stronger checks on the evidence used by the local learner. Your existing rotation, learned adjustments and combat history remain available.

## New — Optional Pick Pocket with Rogue openers

- Enable **Options → Rogue - Stealth openers → Pick Pocket with openers** to attempt learned Pick Pocket before **Ambush, Garrote or Cheap Shot** using their existing secure buttons and bindings.
- The option is **OFF by default**, persists across reload/logout and returns to OFF with **Reset defaults**. It is shown only to Rogues and does not change BASE.
- The optional attempt requires **out-of-combat Stealth and a live hostile target**. Learned localized spell names are used; an unavailable or oversized optional line is omitted without replacing the opener.
- Every opener remains **player-operated**. There is no cast sequence waiting for successful pickpocketing, and the addon never changes your global loot settings.

**Check Auto Loot and its modifier key.** The macro attempts Pick Pocket but cannot guarantee that loot is collected before the opener. A resisted attempt can compromise Stealth and prevent a Stealth-only opener. Keep this convenience disabled if you prefer not to add that risk to a Hardcore pull.

**Example:** your Rogue knows Pick Pocket and Ambush and has a main-hand dagger. Enable the option, enter Stealth, move behind an appropriate NPC and manually press the Ambush slot's button or binding. That input attempts Pick Pocket before Ambush. Check the actual loot with your Auto Loot settings; no additional action slot or external program is needed.

## Improved — Better-qualified learning samples for every class

- **Unknown is no longer easy:** an unreadable target level is not interpreted as a trivial enemy. The logger can recover level, classification and maximum health from an opponent actually involved in combat, rather than an unrelated selected target.
- **Late group tags do not teach poor rotation:** if your first meaningful participation occurs after both **4 seconds** and **25% of the fight**, that fight is excluded from comparative DPS/adaptive learning.
- **Participation is class-aware in coverage:** pet activity, outgoing/incoming damage and misses, hostile control/cast starts and effective healing count. Utility casts and pure overheal do not disguise a late entry.
- **Keep the history:** excluded fights retain their raw damage and duration, with explicit quality reasons. Existing historical fights and learned contexts are not rewritten or reset.

For example, spending most of a short group fight Skinning and tagging the enemy near the end should not teach the learner that your ordinary rotation produces very low DPS. Normal energy or control waits after engagement are not rejected as late entry. This is an initial-participation check, not an active-time DPS rewrite or a detector for every idle interval.

## Fixed — Consistent Rogue input hints and tidy Options

- Remove residual Rogue **KEEP SPAMMING / BASE SPAM** guidance from idle and fallback states. With no active target, the Advisor says **SELECT TARGET**; normal pull guidance asks for one BASE press.
- With Smart HUD disabled or in safe mode, **ADVISOR OFF / MANUAL CONTROL** avoids claiming that attacks need restarting. The existing prominent, binding-aware attack-stopped notice and energy/control waits are preserved.
- Keep the new Rogue Options section clear of utility buttons and the footer. **Reset defaults** refreshes the existing secure macros as well as the saved option; protected changes wait until combat ends.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, HUD positions, combat history, tuning data and ON/OFF choices are preserved. Pick Pocket starts disabled unless explicitly enabled.
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2` and telemetry contract `1` remain unchanged. The new sample-quality evidence applies to newly recorded fights.
- Existing action slots/bindings, the DPS/aggro meter and Diagnostic Pixel V3 encoding are unchanged.
- The pixel and any external reader are referenced exclusively for **passive diagnostics**. Protected gameplay actions require the player's click/key press through WoW's secure system; movement, facing and target selection remain manual.
- No external executable, automatic gameplay input or gameplay-data upload is introduced.

Use `/hcob options` for Pick Pocket and the existing `/hcob tuning status` or `/hcob tuning on|off` commands for the learner. Check `/hcob errors` after testing.
