# HCOneButton v1.29.25

## One cast per DoT — no more doubled Corruption or Agony.

> **DoTs and debuffs are no longer recommended twice right after a successful cast.**

Version `1.29.25` fixes a timing gap that hit every class with DoTs or target debuffs. When a spell succeeds, its debuff reaches your client a moment later. In that short gap the Advisor still saw the target without the debuff and showed the same spell again, so a key press (or an external pixel reader that keeps pressing the shown slot) cast it twice.

## Fixed — Double DoT and debuff casts

- **The cast counts right away.** After a successful cast, the debuff is treated as applied for 1.5 seconds on the target you cast it on. As soon as the real debuff appears, its actual timer takes over.
- **Resists are still handled.** If the spell is resisted and no debuff appears, it is recommended again when the window ends.
- **Target switches are safe.** The window belongs to the target you cast on: a new target still gets its own DoTs at once.
- **Classes covered:** Warlock (Corruption, Curse of Agony, Immolate, Curse of Weakness, Fear), Priest (Shadow Word: Pain, Holy Fire), Shaman (Flame Shock), Druid (Moonfire, Rip, Rake, Faerie Fire (Feral), Entangling Roots), Hunter (Hunter's Mark, Concussive Shot, Wing Clip), Mage (Polymorph), Rogue (Garrote) and Warrior (Rend, Hamstring).

**Example:** a Warlock casts Curse of Agony. Before, the Advisor could show Curse of Agony again for a fraction of a second and a second cast went out with 20+ seconds still left on the first. Now the Advisor moves straight to the next spell.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, saved positions, slot order, permanent bindings, combat history and learned adjustments are preserved.
- The Diagnostic Pixel is unchanged, so external pixel readers keep working as before.
- Protected actions still require your click or key press.
