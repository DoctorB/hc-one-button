# HCOneButton v1.29.17

## From Stealth to your first strike.

> **Early-level Rogues no longer get stuck waiting in Stealth for an opener they have not learned yet.**

Version `1.29.17` fixes a gap in Rogue leveling recommendations that can become noticeable around the time you learn Pick Pocket. The Advisor now provides an explicit attack to begin combat when no compatible dedicated opener is available in your learned toolkit.

## Fixed — A practical opening at early levels

- Suggest trained **Sinister Strike**, or **Hemorrhage** when applicable, from Stealth if no weapon-compatible Ambush, Garrote or Cheap Shot has been learned.
- Keep dedicated learned openers ahead of the fallback. A temporary shortage of energy or invalid range still produces a wait, rather than wasting Stealth on a different attack.
- Recognize that a learned Ambush cannot serve as an opener without the required main-hand dagger.
- Keep BASE's Stealth protection: selecting a target does not automatically start an attack.

## Improved — Optional Pick Pocket with early openers

- The existing **Pick Pocket with openers** option now also covers Sinister Strike/Hemorrhage slots when used outside combat from Stealth.
- Ordinary in-combat builder casts skip Pick Pocket. The option remains **OFF by default**, and your saved choice is preserved.
- No castsequence waits for successful pickpocketing. Unlearned Pick Pocket or an oversized optional macro line cannot replace the actual attack.

**Example:** a low-level Rogue learns Pick Pocket but has no dedicated stealth opener yet. After entering Stealth and approaching the target, the Advisor suggests Sinister Strike. Press its action to open combat; if the optional setting is enabled, the macro attempts Pick Pocket first.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, action slots, permanent bindings, combat history and learned adjustments are preserved.
- Each action requires your click/key press. Pick Pocket does not guarantee loot collection before the attack; Auto Loot settings and its modifier still apply. A resisted attempt can compromise Stealth.
- Other class policies, including the Hunter fixes in `1.29.16`, remain unchanged.

## Validation and limitations

- **57 Lua files**, **44 Lua regression suites**, **58 TOC references** and **19 offline release-tool tests** pass; the addon package contains **62 files**.
- **224 Rogue checks**, including **42 new checks**, cover learning progression, enabled/disabled Pick Pocket, weapon requirements, macro conditions and energy/range waits.
- Dedicated positional openers still require you to position correctly. This correction does not add automatic combat execution or claim a measured DPS increase.

## Compatibility Target

- World of Warcraft Classic Era / Hardcore — Interface `11509`
- All nine supported classes; this release focuses on Rogue leveling
- No additional addon or external program required
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2`, telemetry contract `1`, fight schema `14`
