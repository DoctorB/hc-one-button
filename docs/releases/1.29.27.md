# HCOneButton v1.29.27

## Priest pulls wait for you; Warlock DoTs fit the fight from the first second.

> **Selecting a target no longer starts a Priest pull, and Warlock DoTs are skipped on mobs that usually die too fast for them.**

Version `1.29.27` brings the Warlock pull rule of `1.29.22` to the Priest and makes the Warlock DoT gates useful at the start of a fight.

## Fixed — Priest pulled as soon as a target was selected

- **BASE is the decision to pull.** Out of combat, selecting a hostile target now shows `PULL READY / PRESS BASE` with no spell, so nothing is cast and an external pixel reader has nothing to press.
- **Pressing BASE starts the pull.** BASE casts as before (wand, or Smite / Mind Flay without a wand) and the opener (Holy Fire, Mind Blast, Smite or Shadow Word: Pain) then follows on that target.
- **Every pull needs its own press.** Switching to another target before the pull needs a new BASE press, and entering combat clears it, so the next mob waits for BASE again.

**Example:** a level 8 Priest tabs to a Defias Bandit. Before, Smite went out at once and the pull started. Now the panel waits with `PULL READY` until BASE is pressed.

## Improved — Warlock DoTs at the start of a fight

- The time-to-kill estimate needs a few seconds of damage, so at the start of a fight the DoT gates (Corruption 8 s, Curse of Agony 18 s, Immolate 11 s) always let the DoT through, even on mobs that die in a few seconds.
- Until that estimate is ready, the gates now use how long this character usually takes to kill the same mob alone: the median of the latest solo kills (at least 3, up to 7) with mob and player level within one, scaled by the target's current health. The reason text shows `TTK ~Ns hist` when this is used.
- No history for that mob means no change: the DoT is offered as before.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Settings, saved positions, slot order, permanent bindings, combat history and learned adjustments are preserved.
- The Diagnostic Pixel is unchanged, so external pixel readers keep working as before.
- Protected actions still require your click or key press.

## Validation and limitations

- **57 Lua files**, **48 Lua regression suites** (144 Warlock and 125 Priest checks, including the new pull gate and historical kill time checks) and **58 TOC references** pass.
- Checked in game before release: 60 Priest fights at level 8-9 started with `PULL READY` or after the mob engaged, with no death; the Warlock change was checked in game by the author.
- The kill-time history reads only the current character's saved combat history; a new character starts with the old behavior.

## Compatibility Target

- World of Warcraft Classic Era / Hardcore — Interface `11509`
- All nine supported classes
- No additional addon or external program required
- Learner revision `4`, adaptive schema `2`, telemetry contract `1`, fight schema `14`
