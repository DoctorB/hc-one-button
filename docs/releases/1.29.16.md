# HCOneButton v1.29.16

## Keep your Hunter's shots flowing.

> **A focused Hunter leveling update: corrected Auto Shot startup, useful melee recommendations from the first levels, and clearer guidance when you need to stop or adjust distance.**

Version `1.29.16` improves the transition between ranged and melee combat without changing your action slots or asking you to reset your character's settings.

## Fixed — Auto Shot startup and recovery

- BASE now prepares melee **before** enabling Auto Shot. A trailing melee command no longer switches attack mode immediately after starting ranged fire.
- A stopped Auto Shot is no longer reported as active because an older projectile lands afterward. Readable client state takes precedence over cached events.
- Restart hints respect movement, ongoing casts/channels and Auto Shot usability.
- Invalid or unknown ranged distance no longer receives a misleading **ATTACK OK** message. **STOP TO SHOOT** explains when movement is preventing ranged fire.

## Improved — Leveling before and after your first pet

- **Raptor Strike** is available during ordinary melee, not only against nearly defeated enemies. Once queued, its suggestion clears; the secure action avoids toggling off an already queued strike.
- **Arcane Shot** can be suggested outside the short window after Auto Shot, including while moving at valid range. Mana and cooldown checks remain.
- **Aimed Shot and Multi-Shot** require stationary weaving opportunities. Check nearby enemies yourself before using Multi-Shot.
- Learned **Bestial Wrath and Intimidation** remain eligible even when a different talent tree has more points.
- Before you have a living pet, ordinary enemy attention is not treated as lost pet aggro.
- Hunter's Mark respects usability and range. Serpent Sting observations cannot remain active indefinitely after a missed removal event or leak between targets.

**Example:** target a mob, stand at shooting range and press BASE once. Let Auto Shot run and follow individual ability suggestions. If the mob reaches melee, use the suggested melee action; press Raptor Strike only once while it queues. After creating distance, stop moving and use BASE again when the Advisor asks you to resume shooting.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required:** options, positions, deterministic slots, bindings, combat history and learned adjustments are preserved.
- BASE is a start/recovery action, not a continuously spammed key. Ranged fire still requires suitable range and a stationary firing window.
- Emergency controls, pet-care features and other classes retain their existing policies. Protected actions require your click/key press.

## Validation and limitations

- **57 Lua files**, **44 Lua regression suites**, **58 TOC references** and **19 offline release-tool tests** pass; the verified addon package contains **62 files**.
- Dedicated Hunter checks cover secure macro order, learned-rank queue handling, movement, native/event auto-repeat state, melee/ranged reach, talent availability and Serpent Sting event ordering.
- These are behavioral corrections, not a measured DPS increase or automatic combat execution.
