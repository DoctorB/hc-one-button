# HCOneButton v1.29.13

## More fight. Less hesitation.

> **Warrior leveling becomes more offensive: spend Rage sooner, get clearer swing timing and keep control over when to disengage.**

Version `1.29.13` refines Warrior combat from the starting levels onward. Risk warnings still tell you when a fight looks unfavorable, but they no longer replace your offensive rotation with an early escape plan.

## Improved — Keep fighting while assessing the risk

- **Caution is advice, not a forced retreat.** Above your configured Critical HP threshold, low-health warnings, unfavorable fight estimates and multi-pulls keep the available offensive rotation running.
- **You decide when to leave.** Hamstring, Retaliation and Shield Wall remain available through their existing manual actions; preventive warnings no longer force those spells over damage.
- **Critical HP still has priority.** At or below the configured threshold, the Advisor returns to its emergency recommendations. Interrupts and ongoing casts/channels retain their existing priority.
- Useful mitigation still competes with damage, with active debuffs protected from repeated refreshes. Learned Execute, Overpower and core strikes keep their offensive roles.

## Improved — Rage spending that fits your learned abilities

- Before learning Mortal Strike, Bloodthirst or Whirlwind, Heroic Strike uses **your saved base minus 10 Rage, with a minimum of 20**. The standard base of 35 therefore becomes an effective threshold of **25 Rage** for the starting kit.
- Learning one of those core spenders restores the configured base budget. Near a kill without Execute, another 5 Rage is removed, still with a minimum of 20. Cleave requires 5 more than the current Heroic Strike threshold.
- Remove hidden level-based floors and extra Rage requirements caused by danger warnings. Choosing a base of 20 no longer silently becomes 35 against an equal-level enemy.
- The Options label and tooltip explain **Heroic Strike base rage**. Existing saved values are preserved; no reset or new binding is needed.

## Fixed — More usable next-swing guidance

- Heroic Strike and Cleave appear earlier before the next main-hand swing, leaving more time to react after display confirmation. The window normally spans the final **0.80–1.10 seconds**, capped at 65% of a swing for very fast weapons.
- An already queued strike is not requested again. A new swing also clears an outdated, unqueued request immediately.
- Warning colors do not bypass ordinary suggestion stabilization. Real emergencies and interrupts remain immediate.

**Example:** your level-6 Warrior has 25 Rage, Battle Shout is active and Rend is already on the target. With the standard base of 35, Heroic Strike can now appear as the next swing approaches. If the fight forecast becomes unfavorable, the warning remains visible without replacing that attack with PREPARE ESCAPE. You choose whether to keep fighting; reaching your Critical HP threshold still activates emergency advice.

## Upgrade and safety

- **No reset required.** Keep settings, positions, combat history, learned adjustments, action slots and permanent bindings.
- Open `/hcob options` → **Warrior - Combat policy** to review the base Rage value and its tooltip. `/hcob hsrage 20` selects the lowest base; `/hcob critical N` controls the emergency-health boundary within its existing range.
- Learned Execute still preserves Rage between 21–30% target HP, releasing queued spending at 85 Rage. Missing Execute never triggers that reserve.
- This offensive risk policy is **Warrior-only**. Priest, other class rotations and the party-healing panel retain their existing behavior.
- Every protected action still requires your click/key press. Advice is not a guarantee of survival or a measured DPS increase.
